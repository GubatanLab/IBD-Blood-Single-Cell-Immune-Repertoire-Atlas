Figure 6. T–B coordination and inferred reciprocal communication in IBD

(A) Participant-level partial Spearman correlations between TCR and BCR features, adjusted for diagnosis, age, sex, and receptor depth. Asterisks indicate FDR <0.05 (*), <0.01 (**), or <0.001 (***). (B) Helper/regulatory–B-cell program correlations in transcriptome-evaluable IBD (n = 207), adjusted for diagnosis, demographics, lineage depth, inflammation, biologic-response status, and acquisition series. Significance used 5,000 series-blocked permutations with FDR correction. (C) Independent helper/regulatory contributions in joint models. Points and bars show standardized coefficients and robust 95% CIs; FDR correction included 15 terms. (D) Adjusted Tph/Tfh-help–B-cell-antigen-presentation association. Axes show rank residuals; the line is a descriptive fit. (E) Diagnosis-stratified correlations between expanded-TCR cytotoxicity and IgA-mucosal or plasma-differentiation programs, with participant-bootstrap 95% CIs. (F) Leave-one-acquisition-series-out evaluation of helper-only, cytotoxic-only, and joint models. Values correlate pooled held-out predictions with observed scores. Joint-minus-helper improvements were not permutation significant. (G) MultiNicheNet-inferred T–B interactions. Rows summarize B-cell MHC-II toward Tfh or Th17, B-cell CD86 toward Treg/Th17, Treg TGF-β toward IgM plasma, Tfh/Th17 CD40L toward B cells, and Th17 BAFF toward B/plasma cells. Columns show CD or UC inflammation contrasts, shared-series CD versus UC, and S6-restricted disease-control contrasts. Each bubble represents the highest-priority eligible pair within its row and context; area encodes priority and color denotes CD or UC. Exact interaction identities and scores are provided in Source Data. Correlations and inferred interactions do not establish direct signaling, antigen specificity, or causality. See also Figures S7 and S8.
