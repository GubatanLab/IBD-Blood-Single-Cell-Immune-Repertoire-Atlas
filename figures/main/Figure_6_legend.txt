Figure 6. Clonotype-restricted helper states coordinate with systemic B-cell remodeling and nominate bidirectional signaling circuits in IBD. (A) Participant-level partial Spearman correlations between global TCR and BCR repertoire or transcriptional features, adjusted for diagnosis, age, sex, and receptor depth. Asterisks indicate Benjamini-Hochberg FDR <0.05 (*), <0.01 (**), or <0.001 (***). (B) Partial Spearman correlations in pooled IBD between prespecified pathogenic Th17, conventional Th17, suppressive Treg, or Tph/Tfh-help programs and five B-cell response programs. Models adjusted for diagnosis, age, sex, log CD4-cell depth, log B-cell depth, inflammation status, biologic-response status, and acquisition series. P values were obtained from 5,000 acquisition-series-blocked permutations and corrected across the displayed pooled-IBD tests. (C) Independent helper-state contributions from joint pooled-IBD models containing Tph/Tfh help, pathogenic Th17, and suppressive Treg scores. Points show standardized coefficients and bars show heteroskedasticity-robust 95% confidence intervals. Models additionally adjusted for diagnosis, age, sex, CD4-cell depth, B-cell depth, inflammation status, biologic-response status, and acquisition series; FDR was controlled across the 15 displayed helper-by-B-cell terms. (D) Representative adjusted association between the Tph/Tfh-help program and B-cell antigen-presentation program in pooled IBD. Axes show rank residuals after removal of the covariates used in panel B; the line is a descriptive linear fit. The annotation reports the Tph/Tfh coefficient and FDR from the joint model in panel C. (E) Diagnosis-stratified partial Spearman correlations between the expanded-TCR cytotoxicity score and IgA mucosal plasma-cell or plasmablast/plasma-cell-differentiation programs. Points and bars show estimates and participant-bootstrap 95% confidence intervals from models adjusted for age, sex, log TCR depth, and log BCR depth. (F) Leave-one-acquisition-series-out validation of helper-only, cytotoxic-only, and joint models for the two B-cell programs. Each acquisition series was predicted using models trained on all remaining series; displayed values are Spearman correlations between pooled held-out predictions and observed scores. (G) MultiNicheNet prioritization of bidirectional helper-T-cell-B-cell communication programs across inflammation and diagnosis contrasts. Each bubble represents the highest-priority canonical ligand-receptor interaction for the indicated pathway and context; area indicates the MultiNicheNet prioritization score, and color denotes the disease context (CD, blue; UC, orange). Recurrent programs included B-cell MHC-II and CD86 signaling to helper T cells, Treg-derived TGF-beta signaling to IgM plasma cells, and Tfh/Th17-derived CD40L and BAFF signaling to B-lineage states. MultiNicheNet integrates ligand and receptor expression, differential state, prior ligand-receptor knowledge, and predicted ligand-target activity. These cross-sectional, expression-compatible interactions do not establish physical contact, peptide-TCR specificity, signaling flux, causal direction, or mediation.
